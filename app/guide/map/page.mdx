# WildHacks Event Map

## Event Spaces

WildHacks programming will take place across two buildings connected to each other by an indoor bridge.

[Learn about leaving and re-entering event spaces during the event.](/guide/logistics/leaving-the-venue)

<Callout type="error" emoji="🔥">
  Do NOT use rooms in Tech outside of the ones listed below. Failure to abide by our space restrictions will result in
  immediate dismissal from the hackathon.
</Callout>

### Tech

Check-in, workshops, and opening/closing ceremony will take place in Tech. The building is accessible via the main entrance on the west side of the building, which faces Sheridan Road.

### Mudd

The [WildHacks Help Desk](/guide/help-desk) and [WildSnacks Station](/guide/logistics/food-and-drinks) will be located on the second floor of Mudd.

The area will be open 24/7 and will include [overnight spaces](/guide/logistics/sleeping-and-showering).
