## Ideas

Need a starting point? The prompts below are some ideas, not requirements. Use
one, combine a few, or do something completely different!
- **Play your wildcard**: Take this year's theme literally or not: luck, chance,
  strategy, bluffing, or a card game of your own.
- **Campus life**: Make one part of student life easier, from finding a study
  spot to splitting costs with roommates.
- **Your community**: Build a tool for a neighborhood, local business, or group
  you are a part of.
- **Learning**: Help people pick up a skill, a language, or a tough class more
  effectively.
- **Wellbeing**: Support better habits, sleep, focus, or balance.
- **Sustainability**: Help people waste less, use less energy, or understand
  their environmental impact.
- **Accessbility**: Make technology or information easier for people with
  disabilities.
- **Developer tools**: Build something that improve a developer's programming
  experience: a CLI, an editor extension, a debugging aid.
- **Data for good**: Turn a public dataset into something people can actually
  use.

**Building something that already exists?** Familiar ideas are welcome, and
plenty of great hackathon projects start as a twist on an existing app. If
you are building a common type of project, like a to-do app, course planner,
chatbot, or dining menu app, tell the judges what makes yours different: a new
audience, a better approach, or a feature others do not have. Originality is a
part of the judging criteria, so showing what is new matters.
