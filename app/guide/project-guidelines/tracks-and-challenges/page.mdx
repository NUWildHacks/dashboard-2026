## Track and Challenge Rules

Your project can only be submitted to one track. Check out this year's [prizes](/guide/judging-and-awards/prizes) to see what you can win!

However, you can enter your project into any number of MLH's challenges for a chance to win more prizes in addition to the one track!

## Challenge

Our sponsor challenges are with Claude and Appifex. In your submission, identify how you used Claude and/or Appifex to be considered for the challenges.
