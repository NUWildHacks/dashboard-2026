# Leaving the Venue

Feel free to leave the venue at any time to return to your dorm, grab food, or explore Evanston.

Doors to buildings or rooms may be locked after hours. If you are locked out, contact an organizer in
[#ask-organizers](https://discord.com/channels/1340046559298982019/1342775711722901554) on Discord.

Be prepared to show your [WildHacker Code](https://dashboard.wildhacks.net/code) when re-entering.
