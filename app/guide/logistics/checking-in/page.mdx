# Checking In

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚠️">
  **You must be checked in to participate in WildHacks.**
</Callout>

## **When** to Check In

On April 11, 2026, check-in begins at **8:30 AM CT** and will end at the end of opening ceremony. Opening ceremony will begin at **10:00 AM CT**.

<Callout type="info" emoji="🕘">
  Check-in begins at **8:30 AM CT** on April 11, 2026.
</Callout>

## **Where** to Check In

Check in at the **Northwestern University Technological Institute.** Enter through the west entrance off of Sheridan Road.

<Callout type="info" emoji="🗺️">
  **Northwestern University Techological Institute** | 2145 Sheridan Road, Evanston, IL 60208 | [ Google
  Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KvQNxa82V3LPenuh8)
</Callout>

<GuideImage src="tech-institute.jpg" alt="Technological Institute" />

## **How** to Check In

When you arrive at the Technological Institute, turn left and head over to the check-in desk. **Have your student ID AND your [WildHacker Code](https://dashboard.wildhacks.net/dashboard) visible on your phone.** An organizer will scan your code and check your ID. You'll then get some cool WildHacks swag and will be ready to start hacking!

<Callout type="info" emoji="🆔">
  You must bring your student ID and [WildHacker Code](https://dashboard.wildhacks.net/dashboard), available through the
  dashboard.
</Callout>
