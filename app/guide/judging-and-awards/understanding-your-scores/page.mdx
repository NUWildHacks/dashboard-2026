# Understanding Your Scores

After the event ends, you can request your scores and feedback by sending an email to [wildhacks@northwestern.edu](mailto:wildhacks@northwestern.edu).

Each project is judged independently by 3 to 4 judges. We assign judges so no one reviews the same project twice and so
teams are evaluated by a diverse set of reviewers.

The full rubric and weighting live on [Project Evaluation Criteria](/guide/judging-and-awards/project-evaluation).

## Score Normalization

Round 1 raw scores are normalized so strict-versus-lenient judging differences do not unfairly help or hurt teams.
Details are documented on [How We Calculate Scores](/guide/judging-and-awards/scoring-formula).
