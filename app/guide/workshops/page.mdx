# WildHacks Workshop Week

_We heard your feedback!_ Workshop Week is an **ALL-NEW, hybrid, optional** collaboration to introduce first-time WildHackers to software development topics. These sessions are meant to help early-stage WildHackers prepare themselves for the development portion of the hackathon. Please note the following:

<Callout type="error" emoji="❌">
  These workshops will be **hybrid**. Therefore, *no sleeping accommodations will be provided for out-of-town
  WildHackers.* If you are an out-of-town WildHacker, we encourage you to attend the **virtual** option to minimize
  travel.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" emoji="❤️">
  We are very grateful to Northwestern's computing student organizations for hosting these sessions.
</Callout>

## Workshops

### DISC X WildHacks Git/GitHub Workshop

**April 6, 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM**  
In-Person Location: Tech M177  
Virtual Option: https://northwestern.zoom.us/j/92894172756

Join us for a hands-on workshop where you'll learn how to manage your projects, collaborate with others, and start building your developer portfolio! We'll cover all the essentials including how to set up repositories to understanding version control and writing pull requests. This session will give you the foundation you need to work like a pro.

### ColorStack X WildHacks SQL & Databases Workshop

**April 7, 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM**  
In-Person Location: Tech M166  
Virtual Option: https://northwestern.zoom.us/j/96017289357

Join us for a beginner-friendly workshop designed to get you querying with confidence! We'll walk you through how to design tables, write queries, and extract meaningful insights from data. By the end of the session, you'll have a solid understanding of how databases work and how to put them to use in your own projects.

### IMC Trading x WildHacks Tech Talk

**April 8, 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM**  
In-Person Location: Tech LR3

Hear from a Software Engineer and a Hardware Engineer as they share what it's like to build technology at one of the world's leading trading firms. The session will wrap up with an open Q&A, and dinner will be provided. Don't miss this opportunity to learn about engineering life at IMC and explore recruiting opportunities! (https://imc.recsolu.com/app/collect/event/7f7mf8T8lS3kXHAntpLG9A)
