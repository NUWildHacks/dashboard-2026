# WildHacks 2026 Judging Guide

Thank you so much for judging at WildHacks 2026. We're so excited to have you. Here are some details about how judging will work at the hackathon.

Judging takes place on **Sunday, April 12, 2026**.

As a judge, you'll get:

- The opportunity to see some of the most innovative projects from students at Northwestern University and dozens of other universities around the country
- A free meal if you're judging in person
- Free WildHacks swag
- A volunteer judge certificate

<Callout emoji="💖">
  Thank you so much for volunteering your time to help make WildHacks a success. WildHacks is an entirely student-run
  volunteer-based student hackathon that aims to provide a welcoming and inclusive environment for all participants from
  all skill levels for free. We're glad that you can be a part of it.
</Callout>

## How Judging Works

The way judging works is outlined on **[How Judging Works](/guide/judging-and-awards/how-judging-works)**.

You can find the rubric on **[Project Evaluation Criteria](/guide/judging-and-awards/project-evaluation)**.

<Callout type="info">
  Round 1 will be hybrid, with virtual and in-person judges reviewing video submissions. Round 2 will be solely
  in-person, with two rooms of presentations showcasing our top 25 teams from round 1.
</Callout>

## Judging Technical Requirements

- **All judges must have a device with a web browser to access the judging platform.** We recommend using a laptop or desktop as opposed to a smartphone. In-person judges must bring their own device.
- **Remote judges must have access to [Zoom](https://zoom.us/).**
